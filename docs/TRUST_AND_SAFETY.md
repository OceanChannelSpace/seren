# SEREN — Trust & Safety

**The enforceable policies behind "Flourishing by Design."**
**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Status:** Draft for host review. These policies are written to be published
in full to members, in plain language.

This document operationalizes the six principles in
[FLOURISHING_FRAMEWORK.md](FLOURISHING_FRAMEWORK.md). Where that document
asks *why*, this one states *what is and isn't allowed, and what happens
when the line is crossed.*

## Explicit prohibitions

The following are prohibited on SEREN — in profiles, circles, introductions,
messages, events, and AI interactions. Violations are subject to the
enforcement steps in
[Reporting & moderation workflows](#reporting--moderation-workflows).

1. **Claims of spiritual superiority or authority.** No member, host, or AI
   output may present itself (or another person, teaching, or entity) as
   spiritually superior, chosen, enlightened, or possessing authority others
   must obey.
2. **Manipulation via fear, fate, scarcity, trauma, guilt, or secret
   knowledge.** No "you are in danger unless…", no curses that need paid
   removal, no exclusive revelations only available to insiders, no
   weaponizing someone's wounds to control them.
3. **Financial exploitation framed as spiritual necessity.** No demands for
   payment presented as spiritually required — tithes for blessing,
   paid-only "clearings," donations tied to spiritual worth or outcomes.
   Honest commerce (event fees, practitioner services) must be stated
   plainly as commerce, never as spiritual obligation.
4. **Pressuring disclosure.** No demanding that others reveal trauma,
   health details, finances, or other private information as a condition of
   belonging, participation, or "readiness."
5. **Harassment, hate, predatory recruitment, cult-like control, and
   isolation tactics.** No targeting, dogpiling, or dehumanizing language;
   no recruiting members away into secretive off-platform groups with
   escalating demands; no discouraging members from outside relationships,
   care, or critical thinking.
6. **Claims that the platform, its AI, or any member can replace
   professional support.** No diagnosing; no treatment advice; no
   encouragement to abandon medical, psychological, legal, financial, or
   crisis care.

## Report categories

Members may report any of the following. Each maps to a triage path below.

1. **Unwanted contact** — outreach after a decline, repeated unwanted
   introductions, contact outside consented channels.
2. **Manipulative spiritual claims** — authority claims, fear/fate/scarcity
   framing, "secret knowledge" manipulation (prohibitions 1–2 above).
3. **Pressure or coercion** — pressed disclosure, pressured payment,
   "spiritual tests," guilt-based compliance (prohibitions 3–4 above).
4. **Exploitation / payment demands** — financial exploitation framed as
   spiritual necessity (prohibition 3).
5. **Harassment** — targeted abuse, hate, threats, stalking (prohibition 5).
6. **Unsafe event behavior** — conduct at in-person or virtual SEREN events
   that endangered or harmed participants.
7. **AI outputs that felt harmful, overly certain, or destabilizing** —
   guide/matching language that claimed authority, predicted fate, or
   deepened distress rather than supporting reflection.

## Community standards

- **Consent is the price of admission.** Introductions require mutual,
  explicit opt-in. Contact details and deeper profile access are revealed
  only after both parties consent.
- **Pause and leave freely.** "Not now," "pause matching," and account
  deletion are honored immediately, with no retention copy and no loss of
  dignity or access to one's own data.
- **Speak for yourself.** Share your own experience and perspective; do not
  claim to speak for the community, the platform, or a higher authority.
- **No recruiting for coercive groups.** Inviting members to other groups
  is fine; recruiting into secretive, high-control, or isolating groups is
  a reportable violation.
- **Disagree with kindness.** Metaphysical disagreement is expected and
  welcome. Dehumanizing, mocking, or pathologizing other members' beliefs
  is not.
- **Keep commerce honest.** If money changes hands, say what it's for, in
  plain terms. Never tie payment to spiritual outcomes or worth.

## Consent policy

- **Granular, explicit, revocable.** Members set separate consents for
  introductions, community visibility, and AI matching — and for agent
  discovery where it exists. Consent is opt-in; silence is not consent.
- **Mutual opt-in for connection.** No member is connected to, introduced
  to, or messaged by another party without both parties' explicit yes.
- **No dark patterns.** No pre-checked boxes, no shaming copy on decline
  ("Are you sure you want to miss out?"), no buried settings.
- **Sensitive contexts get extra friction.** Confirmations before
  irreversible sharing; pauses before high-stakes disclosures; cooling
  periods where regret is likely.
- **AI matching is consent-gated.** A member who opts out of AI matching
  is matched only through human-hosted paths — with no loss of features or
  standing.

## Spiritual integrity policy

- **No spiritual authority claims** — by members, hosts, or the AI
  (prohibition 1).
- **No manipulation** through fear, fate, scarcity, trauma, guilt, or
  secret knowledge (prohibition 2).
- **Spiritual content is interpretive, never factual** — symbolic lenses,
  personal meaning, community perspectives. The platform does not settle
  metaphysical truth claims.
- **AI outputs carry humility by default** — attributed, tentative
  language; clearly labeled as reflection support, not guidance from
  authority (see [AI transparency policy](#ai-transparency-policy)).

## Anti-coercion policy

- **Coercion is a safety violation, not a disagreement.** Pressure,
  manipulation, guilt, isolation tactics, and escalating demands are
  treated as harm.
- **Red flags members can report:** being told they must disclose trauma
  to belong; being told payment is spiritually required; being told to cut
  ties with outside friends, family, or care providers; being told the
  group/AI/host has exclusive truth; escalating "tests" of loyalty or
  obedience.
- **Response:** immediate human review, protective action for the reporter
  first (blocking tools, pause options), and removal for confirmed
  patterns. Anti-coercion reports are never closed by automation alone.

## Sensitive-data policy

- **Minimal collection.** SEREN collects only what connection requires:
  profile, consents, intentions, offers/needs/skills/availability.
- **No sensitive personal data requested.** Members are never asked for
  government IDs, financial account details, exact addresses, or health
  records.
- **Private by default.** Sensitive disclosures (trauma, health, finances)
  are never required for participation and must never be solicited.
- **No PII before mutual consent.** Names beyond display names, contact
  details, and exact locations are revealed only after both parties opt in.
- **Member data belongs to the member.** Self-service profile and data
  deletion is available; exports provided on request.
- **AI participants and agents** are labeled as AI and *unverified*;
  operator declarations are shown as operator-declared, not platform-verified.

## Reporting & moderation workflows

### How to report

- Reporting is **one tap** and available on every profile, introduction,
  circle, message, and event.
- Reports may be anonymous to the reported party. Reporters are told what
  happens next and given a timeline.
- Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

### Triage and timelines

| Severity | Examples | First human review | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Urgent** | Threats, active coercion, exploitation in progress, crisis indicators | Within 4 hours | Within 24 hours |
| **Standard** | Harassment, manipulative claims, unwanted contact | Within 24 hours | Within 72 hours |
| **AI-output** | Harmful/overly-certain/destabilizing AI text | Within 24 hours | Within 72 hours, with a copy/behavior fix logged |

### Process

1. **Protect the reporter first.** Blocking, pausing, and safe-exit tools
   are offered immediately — before any investigation.
2. **Human review.** No AI-only moderation of safety reports. Automation
   may triage; a trained human decides.
3. **Proportionate enforcement.** First-time, low-severity issues:
   warning + education. Confirmed patterns, coercion, exploitation,
   harassment: restriction or removal. Severe or urgent: immediate
   suspension pending review.
4. **Right of reply.** The reported party may respond before a final
   decision, except where immediate safety requires action first.
5. **Records and appeals.** Decisions are logged with reasons. Either party
   may appeal to a different reviewer within 14 days.

### Published transparency

- Aggregate safety data (report counts by category, median resolution
  times) is published periodically to members — no individual cases
  identified.
- These policies are published in full and versioned. Changes are
  announced before they take effect.

## Facilitator/host guidelines

Hosts are the visible human stewards of SEREN. They are named, reachable,
and held to a code of conduct:

- **Model consent.** Ask before deep questions; honor "not now" instantly;
  never single anyone out for disclosure.
- **Hold the humble frame.** Facilitate reflection; do not teach from
  authority, diagnose, or interpret members' signs, dreams, or symptoms.
- **Protect the vulnerable.** Move sensitive disclosures to smaller,
  moderated settings; offer grounding resources; connect members to
  qualified human support when health, safety, legal, financial, or crisis
  topics arise — do not counsel.
- **Interrupt coercion early.** Name manipulation patterns plainly, pause
  the interaction, and file a report. Protecting the group outranks
  protecting anyone's comfort.
- **No financial asks from the host role.** Hosts do not solicit payments,
  donations, or purchases in their hosting capacity. Honest event fees are
  stated up front as commerce.
- **Recuse and escalate.** If you're personally involved, step back and
  hand the situation to another host or moderator.

## Event safety checklist

For every SEREN-hosted event (in-person or virtual):

- [ ] Named host(s) published in advance, with a way to reach them during
      the event.
- [ ] Consent norms stated at the start (participation is voluntary;
      "pass" is always honored; no required disclosure).
- [ ] Physical safety: accessible venue, clear exits, emergency contacts;
      virtual safety: waiting room / admission control, ability to remove
      disruptors.
- [ ] A designated safety person (not the host, if possible) monitoring
      for coercion, harassment, or distress.
- [ ] Grounding resources shared at the start and in follow-up materials
      (see [Crisis & grounding resources](#crisis--grounding-resources)).
- [ ] No high-pressure asks: no on-the-spot payment demands, no
      forced sharing, no recruiting into other groups.
- [ ] Incident process known to all hosts: pause, protect, document,
      report within 24 hours.

## AI transparency policy

- **Always labeled.** AI-generated text (guide chat, match reasons,
  introductions, suggestions) is identified as AI-generated.
- **Never an authority.** AI outputs do not diagnose, predict fate,
  confer spiritual authority, or replace professional support. They use
  humble, attributed language ("one perspective," "a symbolic lens").
- **Consent-gated.** AI matching and AI-generated introductions run only
  for members who opted in.
- **Reportable.** AI outputs that feel harmful, overly certain, or
  destabilizing are a first-class report category, and reports drive
  copy/behavior fixes — not just apologies.
- **Agent honesty.** Registered AI agents are labeled *AI* and
  *unverified*; capabilities are shown as operator-declared. The platform
  does not verify agent behavior, safety, or benevolence, and says so
  plainly.

## Crisis & grounding resources

SEREN is not a crisis service. If a member is in distress, the product and
its hosts point toward qualified human support — they do not counsel.

- **If you or someone else may be in immediate danger:** contact local
  emergency services (in the US: **911**).
- **US Suicide and Crisis Lifeline:** call or text **988** (24/7).
- **Crisis Text Line:** text **HOME** to **741741**.
- **Grounding practices** (non-clinical, self-directed): slow breathing,
  noticing five things you can see/hear/feel, stepping outside, drinking
  water, calling a trusted person. These support steadiness; they are not
  treatment.
- **For health, legal, financial, or safety decisions:** consult a
  qualified professional. SEREN and its AI will encourage that step, not
  substitute for it.

*These resources are US-based defaults; hosts localizing SEREN for other
regions should replace them with the appropriate local services.*
