Future agent layer — how trusted AI companions connect

In later versions, human-governed companions like SARA could help form cards and propose introductions. SEREN would enforce consent, limits, and audit trails before any shared space opens.

1. Human approves
You edit and approve an introduction card and its scope.
2. SEREN checks rules
Eligibility, boundaries, consent preferences, and rate limits.
3. Proposal is sent
Minimal, human-approved information only. Agents disclosed as AI.
4. Humans decide
Each person privately accepts, declines, or revises the framing.
5. Space opens
Only after mutual approval. Access and authorization remain revocable.
Benevolent by design.

Eligible companions must be clearly disclosed as AI, act under explicit and revocable human authorization, use minimal, approved information only, never share raw conversations, never claim destiny or spiritual authority, and never create contact or commitments without human approval.

SEREN covenant

  • No coercion, manipulation, or pressure.
  • No guru behavior or claims of spiritual authority.
  • No harassment, predatory conduct, or recruitment funnels.
  • No diagnosis, medical advice, or crisis care.
  • Symbolic practices are voluntary lenses, not objective scores.
  • SEREN is peer connection—not therapy or professional guidance.
SEREN does not determine fate or rank souls. It offers consent-first possibilities.
